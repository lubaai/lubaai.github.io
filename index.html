Questions people ask first

What kinds of video work best?

Talking-based video: podcasts, interviews, webinars, lectures and streams. Clear audio gives the best results.

Can I change what Luba picks?

Yes. Move the start and end points, edit the transcript, or skip a suggestion entirely.

Which file types can I upload?

Most video and audio formats, up to 10 GB per file.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Audio-only recordings get captions and a visual background.
